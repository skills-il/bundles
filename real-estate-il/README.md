# Real Estate & Housing IL

Housing is the single largest line item in most Israeli households' budgets, and the bureaucracy around it is fragmented across municipalities, banks, RMI, the electricity supplier of the week, and 4 different rental marketplaces. This bundle gives you working answers in Hebrew and English, with concrete checklists for every step of buying, renting, or just paying less for the apartment you already live in.

## Key facts

- **Average apartment price in Israel (Q1 2026)**: roughly **2.2M NIS** nationally, **3.4M NIS** in Gush Dan, **1.9M NIS** in the periphery (CBS housing index).
- **First-time buyer mortgage cap**: up to **75% LTV** with proof of equity. Second apartment is capped at **50% LTV**.
- **Rental deposit limit**: by law (Fair Rental Law, 2017), landlords can hold up to **3 months' rent** or one-third of total contract value, whichever is lower.
- **Arnona discounts**: **25% to 90%** depending on category (single parent, student, oleh, reservist, disability). Most eligible households never apply.
- **RMI tenders**: **Mehir Lemishtaken** and **Dira Behanacha** offer new-build apartments **15% to 25%** below market, via lottery, with a 3-5 year wait.
- **Electricity market**: opened to competition in 2020. Private suppliers (Cellcom Energy, Pazgas, Amisragas, others) offer **6% to 15%** off the IEC base rate.

## What you get

1. **Israeli Mortgage Comparator**: build a recommended track mix (prime / kalatz / fixed-shekel), simulate monthly payments across the 5 big banks at current Bank of Israel rates, and decide whether you need a broker.
2. **Israeli Apartment Hunting**: Yad2, Madlan, Komo, Facebook groups, common scam patterns, agency fees, deposit rules, and a neighborhood-by-neighborhood guide.
3. **Israeli Real Estate**: nadlan transactions, mas rechisha, tabu registration, ownership transfers, and the gov.il flows for buyers and sellers.
4. **Israeli Rental Agreements**: review a chuzeh shchirut for unusual or illegal clauses, generate a tenant-friendly contract, and understand the Fair Rental Law boundaries.
5. **Israeli Arnona Optimizer**: check your eligibility for arnona discounts against your specific municipality's bylaws, and print the exact form numbers you need.
6. **Israeli Land Tenders**: track active RMI tenders (Mehir Lemishtaken, Dira Behanacha), filter by eligibility, and walk through the bid submission process.
7. **Israeli Utility Rates Comparator**: run your last 12 months of electricity and water bills against current market offers and tell you if switching saves real money.

## How it works

1. **Buying?** Start with Mortgage Comparator to figure out the financing envelope, then Apartment Hunting for the search, then Real Estate for the actual transaction (tabu, mas rechisha, ownership transfer). Land Tenders is the parallel track if you have patience for a 3-5 year wait.
2. **Renting?** Apartment Hunting for the search, then Rental Agreements to vet the contract before you sign anything.
3. **Already living somewhere?** Arnona Optimizer to lower your monthly bill, Utility Rates Comparator to switch electricity suppliers if it's worth it.
