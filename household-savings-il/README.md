# Household Savings Israel Bundle

Israeli cost of living is not one big number, it is a few dozen small ones that nobody re-checks: the same basket at a different chain, a car insurance policy that auto-renewed, a cellular plan whose promotional rate ended two years ago, a subscription nobody uses. This bundle attacks those line by line, using real published data rather than guesswork.

## Key facts

- **Supermarket prices are public by law**. Every Israeli chain must publish machine-readable price feeds under the Price Transparency Law (חוק שקיפות מחירים). The grocery skill parses those XML feeds directly from the live platforms, so a basket comparison is grounded in the chains' own published prices, not in scraped estimates.
- **Insurance has an official comparison route**. The Capital Market Authority runs a car-insurance calculator at `car.cma.gov.il`, and the bundle also covers the private platforms (Hova.co.il, Shukabit, Wobi, Bestie) across more than 20 insurers.
- **Coupon codes are verified, never invented**. Each code reported comes from a live web search with its source and date, and is checked for expiry, minimum cart, new-customer-only status, and stacking rules.
- **Telecom switching is a real lever**. Number portability, contract versus no-contract pricing, eSIM support, and retention-department negotiation are all covered across Partner, Cellcom, Pelephone, HOT, Golan Telecom, Bezeq, Yes, and the MVNOs.

## Who is this for?

- **Households trying to bring the monthly spend down** without changing how they live
- **Anyone whose insurance or cellular contract is up for renewal** and does not want to auto-renew blind
- **Shoppers about to make a large purchase** who want the real cheapest total, coupon included
- **People building a monthly budget** with Israeli-specific costs (mashkanta, arnona, health tax)
- **Anyone who suspects they are paying for subscriptions they forgot about**

## What you get

1. **Israeli Grocery Price Intelligence**: cross-chain basket comparison from the mandatory price feeds, plus promotion terms and price-trend tracking
2. **Israeli Product Price Comparator**: electronics, appliances, and consumer goods across Zap, KSP, iDigital, Ivory and others, including parallel import versus local
3. **Israeli Insurance Comparator**: car (hova, makif, third-party), home, and supplementary health across 20+ insurers via official and private calculators
4. **Israeli Telecom Comparator**: cellular, internet, TV, and triple bundles across every Israeli provider, with portability and negotiation guidance
5. **Israeli Smart Saver**: cashback programs, credit-card benefits, loyalty stacking, seasonal timing, and a subscription audit
6. **Israeli Coupon Code Finder**: one verified, ranked pass over the Israeli coupon and cashback sources for a specific checkout
7. **Israeli Budget Planner**: the monthly picture, with Israeli inputs (mashkanta tracks, arnona, health costs, Bank of Israel prime)

These skills compare published offers and compute figures from them. They are informational and are not a substitute for advice from a licensed insurance agent or financial professional on your specific situation.
