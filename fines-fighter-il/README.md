# Fines Fighter IL Bundle

Every Israeli has a parking ticket story. Every renter has fought a vaad bayit charge. Every arnona bill has a line that feels arbitrary. This bundle gives you the tools to push back, with properly drafted appeals, correct legal grounds, and realistic expectations.

## Key facts

- **Parking appeals**: the municipality must respond within **90 days** (Paragraph 8A of the Regulations to the Traffic Ordinance). If they don't, the fine is automatically cancelled.
- **Arnona discounts** (single parent, student, disability, new immigrant) can be requested **retroactively up to 2 years** in most municipalities, under the Arnona Discount Regulations (תקנות ההסדרים, הנחה מארנונה).
- **Bituach Leumi appeals** must be filed to the ve'adat irurim within **30 days** of receiving the decision. Win rate on well-documented appeals is materially higher than on ad-hoc letters.
- **License points** are automatically removed **after 2 years** with no further offenses (פקודת התעבורה, תקנה 549). This bundle does not dispute points themselves; it appeals the underlying ticket.
- **Rental disputes**: the Tenant Protection Law and the Fair Rental Law 2017 give renters specific rights against illegal landlord charges, deposit-misuse, and unwritten vaad bayit assessments.

## Who is this for?

- **Drivers** dealing with parking fines, red-light tickets, and traffic court decisions
- **Homeowners and renters** disputing arnona bills and illegal vaad bayit charges
- **Anyone who received a decision from Bituach Leumi** and wants to file a ve'adat irurim appeal
- **Renters** facing illegal landlord charges or deposit disputes

## What you get

1. **Israel Fine Checker**: look up any open traffic or parking fines by license plate or ID and see the reasons given.
2. **Israeli Fines Fighter**: draft formal appeal letters in correct Hebrew legal style, with the right grounds.
3. **Israeli Arnona Optimizer**: check municipal discount eligibility (single parent, student, disability, new immigrant) and request retroactive refunds.
4. **Israeli Bituach Leumi**: understand benefit decisions and file a ve'adat irurim appeal where one applies.
5. **Israeli Rental Agreements**: push back on illegal landlord charges, security-deposit disputes, and undocumented vaad bayit overcharges.

## How it works

1. Start with the lookup skill relevant to your situation: Fine Checker for traffic, Arnona Optimizer for municipal bills, Bituach Leumi for benefits.
2. Pass the details to Fines Fighter (or the domain-specific appeal skill) to generate a formal Hebrew letter with the right legal framing.
3. Submit through the correct channel (online form, email, physical mail) and track the required response timeline.

## What's not in scope

This bundle is built for appeals to municipalities, the traffic court, Bituach Leumi, and landlords. It does not handle consumer credit-report errors, banking fraud disputes, criminal traffic offenses, accidents, or commercial-lease disputes. Those need a different toolkit.

## What to expect

Appeals don't always succeed, but the ones that do save real money. The bundle is designed to help you pick the fights worth fighting and walk away from the ones that aren't, rather than wasting hours on hopeless cases.
